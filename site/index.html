How it works

Every song is scored by smoothed tour frequency × gap multiplier. Tour frequency is how often the song has appeared this tour, smoothed toward last tour's rate. The gap multiplier is estimated from the data — how DMB's chance of playing a song changes with how recently it was played: 1 show ago: ×0.45 · 2 shows ago: ×0.973 · 3 shows ago: ×1.215 · 4-6 shows ago: ×1.347 · 7-12 shows ago: ×1.162 · 13+ shows ago: ×1.205. The famous “almost never back-to-back” rotation shows up as the low multiplier at gap 1.

Opener, set closer, and encore are chosen from empirical slot pools (score × how often the song appears in that slot). The middle of the set is ordered by each song's median position within main sets this tour (tour-only positions order sets twice as well as blending older tours — slotting habits are tour-specific). Set length is the tour median (18 main set songs, 2 encore).

Two sequencing models learned from the data: segue rules — songs whose next song is concentrated in a few followers (e.g. Anyone Seen the Bridge → Too Much 100%, Pantala Naga Pampa → Rapunzel/Pig) must be followed by one of them, inserting it if needed; and encore grammar — encore order comes from encore-opener vs encore-closer propensity (Peace on Earth has opened all 16 of its encores; Two Step and Watchtower close).

Percentages are the model's estimate that the song is played tonight (not that it lands in that exact slot). Backtested on the last 10 shows of this tour: 37% of played songs predicted (vs 29% for a naive most-played-songs baseline), average rank correlation of song order 0.28. Data: dmbalmanac.com.
