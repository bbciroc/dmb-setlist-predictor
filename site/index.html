How it works

Every song's probability comes from an empirical hazard table trained on 322 shows (2021–2026): P(played | frequency tier, gap since last played), with interactions learned from the data — core songs repeat back-to-back 77% of the time while regular rotation songs do so only 20%, and songs with a stable personal cycle peak at 51% when 1.2–1.6× overdue vs their own period. Global gap multipliers for reference: 1 show ago: ×0.578 · 2 shows ago: ×1.041 · 3 shows ago: ×1.272 · 4-6 shows ago: ×1.286 · 7-12 shows ago: ×1.093 · 13+ shows ago: ×1.046.

The 20 most probable songs form the prediction (membership-first — backtested better than reserving picks for slot specialists); opener, set closer, and encore are then assigned within that set from empirical slot pools. The middle of the set is ordered by each song's median position within main sets this tour (tour-only positions order sets twice as well as blending older tours — slotting habits are tour-specific). Set length is the tour median (18 main set songs, 2 encore).

Two sequencing models learned from the data: segue rules — songs whose next song is concentrated in a few followers (e.g. Anyone Seen the Bridge → Too Much 100%, Pantala Naga Pampa → Rapunzel/Pig) must be followed by one of them, inserting it if needed; and encore grammar — encore order comes from encore-opener vs encore-closer propensity (Peace on Earth has opened all 16 of its encores; Two Step and Watchtower close).

Percentages are the model's estimate that the song is played tonight (not that it lands in that exact slot). Rolling backtest over the last 20 shows of this tour: 37% of played songs predicted (vs 27% for a naive most-played-songs baseline), average rank correlation of song order 0.39.
